# Tutorial Script — How to Log In to NovaxPOS

**Length:** ~60–75 seconds
**Category suggestion:** Getting Started
**Style:** Friendly tutorial voice, screen-share

---

## Scene 1 — Intro (0:00–0:10)
**On screen:** Browser open on a blank tab.

> Welcome to NovaxSuites POS! In this quick video, I'll show you how to log in to your account so you can start managing your business.

## Scene 2 — Reaching the login page (0:10–0:30)
**On screen:** Type the store's web address, land on the login page.

> First, open your browser and go to your store's NovaxSuites POS address — this is the link you received when your account was created. You can log in from any device: your computer, tablet, or phone.
>
> This is your login page.

## Scene 3 — Entering credentials (0:30–0:55)
**On screen:** Click the email field, type an email, then the password. Point at the show/hide password icon if present. Click Login.

> Enter the email address and password for your account. If you're a staff member, use the login your manager created for you.
>
> Double-check your password — and remember it's case-sensitive. Then click Log in.

## Scene 4 — You're in (0:55–1:10)
**On screen:** Dashboard loads. Slow pan over the dashboard.

> And that's it — you're on your dashboard, where you can see your sales, stock, and reports at a glance.
>
> If your password isn't accepted, use the Forgot Password link to reset it, or ask your administrator to reset it for you.
>
> That's how easy it is to log in. See you in the next tutorial!

---
