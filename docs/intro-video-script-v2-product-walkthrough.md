# NovaxPOS — Landing Page Intro Video Script

**Length:** ~4½ minutes (≈700 words at a natural speaking pace)
**Style:** Founder speaking over screen-share
**Tip:** Record the screen actions first, then narrate over them — it's much easier than doing both at once.

---

## Scene 1 — Opening (0:00–0:30)
**On screen:** Landing page, slow scroll through the hero.

> Hi, I'm Omaru, founder of NovaxPOS. If you run a shop, a pharmacy, a restaurant, or any business that sells products, you know the struggle — sales in one notebook, stock in another, and no real idea what's actually making you money.
>
> I built NovaxPOS to fix that. It's a complete point-of-sale and business management platform that runs in your browser — nothing to install, works on any device. Let me show you around.

## Scene 2 — The POS (0:30–1:20)
**On screen:** Open the POS screen. Scan or click 2–3 products, apply a discount, take a split payment, print/show the receipt.

> This is the heart of the system — the point of sale. Watch how fast this is: I scan a barcode, or just search by name, and it's in the cart. Keyboard shortcuts for everything, so your cashiers fly through a queue.
>
> Customer wants to pay half cash, half card? Split payments are built in. Hit complete, and the thermal receipt prints instantly.
>
> Every sale updates your stock and your reports in real time — no end-of-day reconciliation, no double entry.

## Scene 3 — Inventory & Warehouses (1:20–2:10)
**On screen:** Products list → stock levels → low-stock alert → warehouse transfer screen.

> Behind the counter, inventory is where most businesses bleed money. NovaxPOS tracks every unit across unlimited warehouses. When stock runs low, you get an alert before the shelf is empty — and batch expiry tracking means nothing goes bad in the back room unnoticed.
>
> Got more than one location? Transfer stock between warehouses in a few clicks, and run reports per location so you know exactly which branch is performing.
>
> You can also generate and print barcodes and QR codes for all your products, right from the system.

## Scene 4 — Analytics & Reports (2:10–2:50)
**On screen:** Dashboard with charts → top-selling products report → profit report.

> Now, my favourite part — knowing your numbers. The dashboard gives you revenue, profit margins, and top-selling products at a glance, updated live.
>
> Want to know which cashier sold the most this week, which product line has the best margin, or what your expenses look like against sales? It's all here, in a couple of clicks. This is the visibility most small businesses never have — and it changes how you make decisions.

## Scene 5 — Running the Business (2:50–3:40)
**On screen:** Quick flips through: purchases/invoices → customer profile with history → user roles screen → returns.

> NovaxPOS covers the whole operation, not just the till. Create purchase orders and professional invoices, and track what you owe suppliers and what customers owe you.
>
> Every customer gets a profile — purchase history, loyalty points, credit balance — so you can actually build relationships, not just process transactions.
>
> Your team gets their own logins with role-based permissions, and every action is logged. Returns and refunds take seconds, with stock restocked automatically.
>
> And it speaks your language — over twenty languages, multiple currencies, and regional tax rules built in.

## Scene 6 — Marketplace & E-Commerce (3:40–4:10)
**On screen:** Marketplace page → a store listing → (optional) e-commerce sync settings.

> Here's what makes NovaxPOS different: once your POS is set up, you can go live on our marketplace and sell online — customers nearby can discover your products, order, and choose delivery, while your inventory stays perfectly in sync. One stock count, two sales channels, zero manual work.

## Scene 7 — Closing & Call to Action (4:10–4:40)
**On screen:** Pricing page → registration form → end on the logo.

> Getting started takes minutes: register, pick a plan, add your products, and you're selling. There's a plan for every size of business, and you can try it before you commit.
>
> If you're ready to stop guessing and start growing, head to the site and create your account today. And if you get stuck, our video tutorials walk you through every feature, step by step.
>
> Thanks for watching — I'll see you inside NovaxPOS.

---

## Recording checklist

- Use a demo tenant with realistic products and a few days of sales data — empty dashboards look bad on camera.
- 1080p screen recording, hide bookmarks bar and notifications.
- Pause half a second after each click so viewers can follow.
- Speak 10–15% slower than feels natural — it reads as confident.
- Trim silences in editing; total should land between 4:00 and 4:45.
