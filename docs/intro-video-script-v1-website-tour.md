# NovaxPOS — Intro Video, Version 1: Website / SaaS Frontend Tour

**Length:** ~3½–4 minutes (≈600 words)
**Style:** Founder speaking, screen-sharing the public website only (no app login)
**Screens used:** Landing page, marketplace, pricing, tutorials, registration

---

## Scene 1 — Welcome (0:00–0:25)
**On screen:** Homepage hero, cursor still.

> Hi, I'm Omaru, founder of NovaxPOS. In the next few minutes I'll walk you through our website so you know exactly what NovaxPOS is, what it costs, and how to get started — all before you even create an account.
>
> In one sentence: NovaxPOS is a complete point-of-sale and business management platform, with a built-in online marketplace — one platform for everything your business needs.

## Scene 2 — Features Section (0:25–1:15)
**On screen:** Scroll slowly through the features grid, hovering each card as you mention it.

> Right here on the homepage you can see everything included in your subscription.
>
> A lightning-fast POS with barcode scanning and split payments. Inventory management across unlimited warehouses, with low-stock alerts and expiry tracking. Real-time analytics — revenue, margins, top products — on live dashboards.
>
> You also get invoicing and purchasing, customer management with loyalty points, returns and refunds, expense tracking, team accounts with role-based permissions, and support for more than twenty languages and multiple currencies.
>
> Every one of these is included — these aren't paid add-ons.

## Scene 3 — How It Works (1:15–2:00)
**On screen:** Scroll to the "How it works" steps; point at each step as you narrate.

> So how do you actually get going? Four steps, and they're right here.
>
> One — register and choose your plan. Two — configure your POS: add products, set your taxes, invite your team. Three — go live on the marketplace, so customers nearby can find your store online. Four — sell, fulfil, and grow.
>
> And notice the marketplace works for everyone: shoppers can discover local products, build a cart, and choose their delivery. Delivery agencies can apply online, get vetted, and start earning by fulfilling orders. It's an ecosystem, not just software.

## Scene 4 — Marketplace Preview (2:00–2:30)
**On screen:** Click through to the marketplace page, browse a shop and a product page briefly.

> Here's the marketplace itself. Every seller on NovaxPOS gets a storefront like this — your products, your branding, synced automatically with your in-store inventory. When something sells online or at the counter, your stock stays accurate everywhere.

## Scene 5 — Pricing (2:30–3:05)
**On screen:** Pricing page; hover across the plans.

> Pricing is simple and transparent — what you see here is what you pay. Pick the plan that fits your size today; you can upgrade any time as you grow, and there's a trial so you can test everything before committing.
>
> No hidden fees, no per-terminal charges, no surprises at checkout.

## Scene 6 — Tutorials & Support (3:05–3:30)
**On screen:** Tutorials page; scroll through categories, open one video briefly.

> You're never on your own. Our tutorials page has step-by-step videos for every part of the system, organized by category and searchable. And if you need more help, support is a ticket away from inside your dashboard.

## Scene 7 — Registration, Sign In & Close (3:30–4:15)
**On screen:** Click "Get Started", show the registration form. Then scroll back up and point at the "Sign in" link in the navbar. End on the logo.

> When you're ready, click Get Started — registration takes about two minutes. Choose your plan, and your POS is live immediately.
>
> And once you're a customer, coming back is easy: the Sign in link at the top right takes you straight to your dashboard, from any device — at the shop, at home, or on your phone. When you're done for the day, just sign out from your profile menu, and your data stays safe in the cloud until your next session.
>
> That's the tour. Stop juggling notebooks and spreadsheets — run your whole business from one place. Create your account today, and I'll see you inside NovaxPOS.

## Recording checklist

- Record in a clean browser window: hide bookmarks, mute notifications, 1080p.
- Scroll slowly and steadily — fast scrolling looks bad on video.
- Hover or point the cursor at whatever you're currently talking about.
- Have the marketplace populated with a few good-looking demo shops/products before recording.
- Keep total length near 4:00 — trim pauses in editing.

**Version 2** (`intro-video-script-v2-product-walkthrough.md`) goes inside the app — use it as the follow-up video for visitors who want to see the POS in action.
