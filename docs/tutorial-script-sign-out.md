# Tutorial Script — How to Sign Out of NovaxPOS

**Length:** ~45–60 seconds
**Category suggestion:** Getting Started
**Style:** Friendly tutorial voice, screen-share

---

## Scene 1 — Intro (0:00–0:10)
**On screen:** Logged-in dashboard.

> In this quick video, I'll show you how to sign out of NovaxSuites POS safely — an important habit, especially if you share a computer or POS terminal with other staff.

## Scene 2 — Signing out (0:10–0:35)
**On screen:** Move the cursor to the profile menu (top-right), click it to open the dropdown, hover over Sign out, then click it.

> From anywhere in the system, look at the top-right corner of the screen and click on your profile.
>
> A menu opens — click Sign out at the bottom.

## Scene 3 — Confirmation & why it matters (0:35–0:55)
**On screen:** Redirected to the login page.

> You're now back at the login page, and your session is closed. Your data is saved automatically — signing out never loses your work.
>
> Always sign out at the end of your shift or when stepping away from a shared till. It keeps your sales records accurate — every action in NovaxSuites POS is tied to the user who's logged in — and it keeps your business data secure.
>
> That's it! See you in the next tutorial.
